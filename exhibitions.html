Five worlds. One museum.

Travel from distant galaxies to the systems inside the human body, meet extinct animals, shake hands with robotics and explore how science can shape a more sustainable future.

1000+ exhibits · many of them interactive

Cosmology Evolution Biology & medicine Robotics & AI Ecology

Cosmology

The sky above us

Locate constellations and galaxies, learn how early navigators used the stars, and follow solar exploration from early Arab traders and Galileo’s telescope to today’s planetary missions.

Keep exploring

Each exhibition looks at science from a different angle — with enough depth for adults and enough interaction to keep younger visitors curious.

Evolution

Discover how life on Earth changed over time, from Darwin’s framework to the Woolly Mammoth, Dodos and dinosaurs that did not survive.

Biology & medicine

Look from microorganisms to the human body, and trace breakthroughs such as penicillin and the first heart transplant.

Robotics & AI

See how artificial intelligence and robotics are changing homes and industry — and meet Rob the Robot.

Ecology

Understand climate change, extinction and pollution while exploring practical ways we can contribute to a more sustainable future.
