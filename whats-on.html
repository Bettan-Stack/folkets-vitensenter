There’s always something new to discover.

From visiting experts to after-hours adventures and hands-on exhibitions on loan, special events give you another reason to come back.

Family event

Night in the Museum

Get the family together for an exciting night sleeping beside dinosaurs and science displays. Bring your own sleeping bag and get ready to explore the museum after hours.

More happening at the museum

Two more reasons to visit — one looking toward the future of flight, the other showing how wind and solar power can be harnessed.

Lecture series

Visiting Professor of Aeronautics

Prof Sheila Widnall from MIT will deliver three lectures on the development of aeronautics and where the future may lead.

Exhibition on loan

Energetica

On loan from NEMO Science Museum in Amsterdam, Energetica lets visitors experience the power of the elements through solar and wind installations.

General admission is free.

Check opening hours, accessibility, café and visitor information before you arrive.
